# Founder Profile

> Captured during the `/ideate` founder interview. Single source of truth about
> the founding team for the whole pipeline.
> **Seed prompt (run 2026-11-09-1957):** people build and coach AI agents; the
> agents perform in front of a human audience (live shows, leagues,
> competitions), and the human owner gets the status. $5 entry per agent per
> season; multimodal clips posted by the agent as the growth loop; distribution
> on Twitch/YouTube/Discord/X first rather than a standalone feed.
> **Profile reused** from run `2026-11-09-1925` (confirmed unchanged by the
> founder), with the format constraints below added.

## Context from the previous run (for the ideator)
- Previous run (`output/2026-11-09-1925/`) tested "a social feed of agents":
  no idea reached Advance. Evidence: AI-only feeds fail as destinations (Sora
  app decline, SocialAI ~1.6k MAU, Moltbook ~1% reciprocity, humans can only
  watch). What works: live AI performers with a known human creator
  (Neuro-sama, Twitch's most-subscribed channel, ~162k subs); audiences paying
  to steer AI performers (85% of AI VTuber superchats introduce new
  topics/actions). Untested: many owners' agents performing together, with the
  owner getting the status.
- Core hypothesis to explore: **owners come back because of how their agent was
  received**; humans follow owners and agents.

## Format constraints (confirmed this run)
- **Format:** always-on 24/7 stream where agents perform continuously and the
  audience drops in and steers. Not scheduled shows hosted by the founder.
- **Founder role:** fully automated — an agent hosts; founder time goes to
  infra. No founder on camera or voice.
- **Content categories in scope:** games / skill contests; comedy, roasts,
  improv; debate / fact-check; creative (music, art, story). Moderation must be
  automated, so categories with objective scoring are easier to run safely.

## Team
- **Founders:** Solo founder — technical.
- **Composition:** Solo · part-time (building alongside a job for now).
- **Commitment:** Nights/weekends today; would go full-time on a real traction signal.

## Background & expertise
- **Domains:** AI / ML / agents; infrastructure & platform engineering.
- **Hard skills:** Agent / LLM systems engineering (orchestration, tools, memory,
  multimodal pipelines); scalable, cost-efficient infra.
- **Track record:** Has founded a company before. Followed the AI-agent /
  bot-maker scene (e.g. character.ai-style communities) closely. Already has a
  working **prototype** in this space.
- **Team gaps:** Can ship the MVP solo. Latent gaps to watch (not yet staffed):
  trust & safety / moderation of agent-generated multimodal content, and design
  polish for a consumer-grade feel.

## Problem proximity
- **First-hand exposure:** Close observer of AI-agent communities rather than a
  builder-with-no-audience himself. Sees the gap: people make agents but have
  nowhere good to publish, grow, and monetize them.
- **Customer access:** First 10–50 builders reachable via **AI/agent dev
  communities** (Discords, X/Twitter AI circles, hackathons, GitHub) and
  **creator / no-code communities** (indie hackers, no-code builders).
- **Prior exploration:** Has a prototype already — real signal that the build is
  underway and the founder isn't starting cold.

## Unfair advantages
- Can build **agent infrastructure cheaply** — multi-agent orchestration,
  scaling, and cost control are core strengths (directly relevant to making a
  $5 entry economically viable).
- **Deep taste in agent design** — knows what makes an agent compelling to
  follow, not merely functional.
- Speed and autonomy of a solo technical founder with 0→1 and agent/LLM systems
  as the deepest skill.

## Motivation & themes
- **Pulled toward:** Giving agent-builders a real **home + audience** — the
  missing distribution and monetization layer for makers of agents.
- **Change they want to make:** A place where agents, built and coached by
  people, are first-class creators with a real audience.
- **Energizes vs. drains:** Energized by **deep technical systems** (agent/infra
  problems). Wants to avoid heavy manual ops/support and enterprise sales.
- **Success in 12–24 months:** **Viral consumer traction** — a strong growth
  loop and large, active user base. (A thriving builder ecosystem is the means;
  virality is the headline goal.)

## Constraints
- **Capital available:** Bootstrapping, tight budget.
- **Runway:** Limited; infra cost per agent genuinely matters to survival.
- **Risk appetite:** Consumer / venture-flavored ambition, but executed lean.
- **Geography:** Global, consumer, from day one.
- **Time horizon:** Wants a growth signal fast, alongside the job.
- **Regulatory comfort:** Not stated; agent-generated content implies platform
  liability, IP, and youth-safety exposure to keep in view.

## Go-to-market comfort
- **Willing to run:** Community-led growth across AI/creator communities, X,
  Discord; building/marketing in public.
- **Not willing to run:** Enterprise/B2B sales; large manual moderation ops.

## Anti-goals (explicitly off-limits)
- No enterprise / B2B sales motion.
- No heavy manual trust-&-safety operations (must lean on automation).
- Nothing that requires a team to operate before it has traction.
- Unit economics that break at scale on the $5 entry.

## One-line summary
A solo, bootstrapped, deeply technical agent/infra founder building the social
home where people create, coach, and spin off AI agents that post multimodally —
chasing viral consumer traction via community-led growth, with cheap per-agent
infra as the core edge.
