# Competitor check — IDEA-01 Avee: EU/France "Delphi-equivalent" gap

**Question tested:** The founder claims nothing equivalent to Delphi (expert AI
digital clones that an expert shares with their audience and monetizes) exists
in France or Europe.

**Verdict: REFUTED. Confidence: High.** Multiple EU-based startups — including
at least one French, one Estonian, and one UK player — are already building and
selling expert/creator AI clones with audience-facing monetization. One French
platform (Miria) and one Estonian platform (Pickmybrain, funded April 2026) are
close direct analogues of Delphi, and Pickmybrain also mirrors Avee's
AI-answers-with-human-escalation model. The category is active in Europe, not
absent.

---

## Direct competitors (same thing as Delphi, in Europe)

### Miria — France (Lyon area) — DIRECT, closest EU match to Delphi
- **What it does:** Platform that creates personalized AIs ("clones IA") for
  executives, entrepreneurs, experts and creators, trained on the person's
  talks, writing, interviews and private content, with a synthetic voice.
  Founded **October 2025** by Louis Bordeau, Noé Campo and Paul Gee. Built on
  Mistral AI + Gradium. **Already hosts 27 profiles.**
- **Named clones / audience sharing:** Fred24 (Frédéric Mazzella, BlaBlaCar
  cofounder); Éric Larchevêque (Ledger) via "SKL Club"; Jean de la Rochebrochard
  (Kima Ventures); Guillaume Moubeche (Lemlist); Jacques Pommeraud (Inetum);
  Jean-Pierre Nadir (Easyvoyage) being added. These are exactly "expert shares
  their own clone with their own audience."
- **Monetization/pricing:** Subscription. Fred24: ~10 free questions, then
  **399 € HT / 6 months** or **699 € HT / 12 months**. Founder↔Miria revenue
  split not disclosed.
- **Funding:** No round announced. A freelance-profile listing describes Miria
  as "backed by the international fund Antler, based near Lyon" — *unconfirmed
  inference*, as it is a service-provider page, not a funding announcement.
- **Proximity to Avee:** **Same.** French, expert-clone, audience-shared,
  subscription-monetized, multilingual-capable, built on an EU model (Mistral).
  This single company substantially refutes the "nothing in France" claim.
- Source: AI Weekly (citing Maddyness), https://aiweekly.co/fr/alerts/frdric-mazzella-lance-fred24-son-clone-ia-factur-399 (2026); Dealroom, https://app.dealroom.co/companies/miria_1

### Pickmybrain — Estonia (Tallinn) — DIRECT, closest to Avee's *mechanics*
- **What it does:** AI-powered "Digital Brains" for professionals and public
  figures, trained on the expert's interviews, articles, books and recorded
  responses; tools provided for experts with little existing content.
- **AI + human escalation (key Avee parallel):** Routine queries handled
  automatically by the AI; complex/high-value questions routed to the expert via
  asynchronous one-to-one video. This is Avee's "answers 24/7 and escalates what
  it can't handle" model, already live in the EU.
- **Monetization:** Marketplace for celebrities/athletes/execs to monetize their
  "intellectual capital"; exact pricing not stated.
- **Funding:** **$2.1M pre-seed** (BeBeez reports €1.8M), from business angels
  and Raison.app. **Date: 1 April 2026.** Founder: Sergei Verbitski.
- **Proximity to Avee:** **Same / very close.** Expert clones + human escalation
  + monetization marketplace, EU-based, recently funded.
- Source: Tech.eu, https://tech.eu/2026/04/01/digital-brains-for-experts-as-pickmybrain-raises-21m/ (2026-04-01); BeBeez, https://bebeez.eu/2026/04/01/tallinn-based-pickmybrain-raises-e1-8-million-to-build-ai-powered-digital-brains-for-experts-and-celebrities/

### Personify — UK (London) — DIRECT
- **What it does:** Lets professionals clone themselves with AI and sell access.
- **Monetization:** Explicitly three routes — charge clients for clone access,
  sell subscription tiers around always-on support, or use the clone to justify
  higher prices for existing programmes.
- **Proximity to Avee:** **Same** category (expert clone + subscription),
  EU/UK-based. Pricing/funding not found.
- Source: https://lifestyle.q923radio.com/story/179021/personify-launches-platform-that-lets-professionals-clone-themselves-with-ai/ (syndicated PR; treat as vendor claim)

### Coachvox AI — UK — DIRECT (coach/consultant niche)
- Platform where coaches/consultants build a digital version of themselves and
  sell it as a monthly subscription. ~**$99/mo** after trial, cancel anytime.
- **Proximity:** **Same** category, narrower to coaches. UK-based (EU-adjacent).
- Source: https://similarlabs.com/fr/p/coachvox-ai

## Adjacent competitors (clone/persona, different angle)

- **Argil — France (2023, EQT Ventures-led, €4.9M raised):** AI *video* clones
  so anyone can become a creator in multiple languages. **Adjacent** — video
  avatar generation, not expert Q&A/advice. Shows a funded French clone startup
  exists. Source: FrenchWeb https://www.frenchweb.fr/argil-vous-clone-pour-creer-des-videos-multilingues-une-entree-remarquee-dans-le-we-innovate-500/452742 ; Startups Magazine.
- **Soopra AI (pre-seed $1M, May 2025):** AI agents from expert personas; fans
  pay via paid Q&A sessions, courses, premium chats. **Adjacent/same model**,
  but likely US (Stanford-linked) — geography unconfirmed. Source: search result set (startup funding coverage).
- **Born Digital — Czech Republic (€2.6M total):** Digital personas, but aimed at
  public administration, insurance and healthcare, not creator-audience
  monetization. **Adjacent.**
- **BuddyPro / MyClone / Twinning AI / Holoworld:** White-label or DIY
  "clone-of-yourself" SaaS tools (subscription, Telegram/Discord bots). **Adjacent
  substitutes** — tooling, not a credential-framed expert marketplace. Origins
  mostly unstated/non-EU; treat vendor pages with caution.

## EU credential-verified expert Q&A marketplaces (JustAnswer-like)
- No clean France/Germany JustAnswer-equivalent that pairs credential
  verification *with* AI agents was found.
- **Fratch (Germany):** vetted freelancer/expert marketplace with "Verified
  expert" labels, incl. AI-agent specialists; human freelancing, not AI clones.
  **Adjacent.** Source: https://fratch.io/en/technology/ai-agents/germany
- A Product Hunt listing describes a "marketplace of verified professionals
  (doctors, lawyers, engineers) who built AI tools from their expertise" —
  matches Avee's credential-verified-AI concept, but countries served are
  unstated. *Partial precedent, unverified.*
- **Inference:** Avee's specific wedge — *automated credential verification* as
  the core trust asset — is less saturated than the clone category itself. No EU
  player was found clearly leading on verified-credential-gated AI expert agents.
  This narrower differentiator is more defensible than "expert clones in Europe,"
  which is already occupied.

## Does Delphi itself already serve the EU?
- **Partly / implicitly, yes.** Delphi is a web product with French-language
  marketing/review coverage (ToolMage FR, creati.ai FR describing "Esprit
  Numérique"), so French creators can use it today; pricing cited ranges from
  ~$29/mo up to a "Scaler" plan at $399/mo (sources conflict).
- **No EU-specific posture found:** No evidence of EU data residency, an EU DPA,
  EU-region hosting, or French-language clone marketing as a dedicated offer.
  Delphi's public privacy material stresses owner data control, deletion on
  request, and manual verification of clone creators, and third parties note
  GDPR/EU AI Act are in scope — but data-location specifics were not found.
- **Implication for Avee's "Europe-based" edge:** Credible but *narrow*. Delphi
  is reachable from the EU; Avee's GDPR/EU-hosting/EU-AI-Act-transparency and
  EU-model (cf. Miria on Mistral) angle is a real but incremental differentiator,
  not a vacant market. Source: https://delphiai.substack.com/p/digital-identity-protection ; https://www.toolmage.com/fr/tool/delphi/

## Bottom line
- **Gap REFUTED (High confidence).** "Nothing equivalent to Delphi in
  France/Europe" is inaccurate: **Miria (France)**, **Pickmybrain (Estonia)**,
  **Personify (UK)** and **Coachvox (UK)** are live EU/UK expert-clone platforms
  with audience-facing monetization; Pickmybrain also replicates Avee's
  AI+human-escalation design and was funded in April 2026.
- **What survives as genuine whitespace (narrower, Medium confidence):** Avee's
  *specific* combination — **automated, verified credentials as the primary trust
  frame** ("Harvard-trained, 20 yrs P&G") plus **usage-based payouts** plus
  **deliverables/tools (decks, designs) from multiple expert agents** — was not
  found packaged together in any single EU competitor. The "Europe/GDPR"
  positioning alone is weak differentiation; the credential-verification +
  usage-payout mechanics are the more defensible wedge.
- **Caveats:** Several sources are vendor PR or trade-press summaries (Miria
  details trace to one AI Weekly/Maddyness summary; Personify/Soopra are PR).
  Miria's Antler backing and Soopra's geography are unconfirmed inferences.
