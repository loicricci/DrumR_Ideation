## Team
- **Founders:** Solo founder, technical.
- **Composition:** Solo, part-time (building alongside a job for now).
- **Commitment:** Nights and weekends; would go full-time on a real traction signal.

## Background & expertise
- **Domains:** AI / ML / agents; infrastructure & platform engineering.
- **Hard skills:** Agent / LLM systems engineering (orchestration, RAG, memory,
  multi-model routing, multimodal pipelines); scalable, cost-efficient infra.
- **Track record:** Has founded a company before. Has **already built Avee V2**
  (v0.6.0, early access): Next.js 16 + FastAPI, RAG on Postgres/pgvector,
  semantic memory, multi-model routing, SSE chat with context pruning and
  auto-summaries, public/private agents, human escalation, Supabase auth,
  image/video/voice generation, Telegram bot, 50+ prebuilt demo agents, and a
  full brand and design system. It grew out of the "Gabee" Streamlit digital-twin PoC.
- **Team gaps:** Trust & safety for expert-advice content; payments, payouts and
  tax compliance for a two-sided marketplace (not built yet); growth/marketing.

## Problem proximity
- **First-hand exposure:** Observer of AI-agent and creator communities; built
  a digital twin of himself (Gabee) before building Avee.
- **Customer access:** AI/agent dev communities (Discord, X, GitHub, hackathons),
  creator / no-code / indie-hacker communities. The founder named no expert
  segment with privileged access ("something else").
- **Prior exploration:** Gabee PoC, then Avee V2. **Traction today: none / under
  50 users.** Waitlist and login are open, but there's no meaningful usage yet.

## Unfair advantages
- Cheap agent infrastructure: per-message inference cost and model routing are
  the founder's core strengths, and they're the variable this revenue model hinges on.
- The product is already built. The bottleneck is demand and monetization, not engineering.
- Taste in agent design.

## Motivation & themes
- **Pulled toward:** Giving experts and creators an AI agent that earns for them
  while they're away ("Avee set you free").
- **Change they want to make:** Experts earn passively from their knowledge,
  paid by real usage of their agent.
- **Energizes vs. drains:** Energized by deep technical systems; avoids manual
  ops/support and enterprise sales.
- **Success in 12–24 months:** Viral consumer traction with a strong growth loop
  (unchanged).

## The revenue model under test (founder's words, this run)
> "From day one, revenue is generated by the usage of each agent. Example:
> User A has 2 agents, K and G. User B pays a $20/month subscription and spends
> 50% of his time on agent K. At any moment the platform is generating at least
> 50% net margin on the usage. So User A receives from agent K's usage by User B
> at least $5."

## Constraints
- **Capital:** Bootstrapping, tight budget. **Runway:** limited; infra cost matters.
- **Risk appetite:** Consumer / venture-flavored ambition, executed lean.
- **Geography:** Global from day one (which implies VAT/GST and cross-border payouts).
- **Time horizon:** Wants a growth signal fast.
- **Regulatory comfort:** Not stated. Expert-advice agents (health, finance,
  legal coaches) and creator payouts (KYC, tax reporting) bring exposure.

## Go-to-market comfort
- **Willing to run:** Community-led growth, building in public, X/Discord.
- **Not willing to run:** Enterprise/B2B sales; large manual moderation ops.

## Anti-goals
- No enterprise / B2B sales motion.
- No heavy manual trust-&-safety operations.
- Nothing that needs a team to operate before traction.
- Unit economics that break at scale.

## One-line summary
A solo, bootstrapped technical founder with a working expert-agent marketplace
(Avee V2, pre-traction) testing whether a $20/month all-access subscription,
paid out to experts by usage share, can grow virally and keep the platform
profitable on every message.
