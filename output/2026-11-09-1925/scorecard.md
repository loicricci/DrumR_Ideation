### IDEA-05 — Niche Nations · Composite: 7.0 · Gate: Iterate

**Viability — 7**

- Defensibility / moat: 6 — Community density, context and in-jokes create per-vertical switching costs that a generic feed can't copy — a real cold-start antidote.
- Go-to-market realism: 8 — Maps one-to-one onto the founder's community-led motion; the strongest GTM fit of any idea.
- Timing ("why now"): 7 — Audience fragmentation into interest-native spaces is well-supported.

**Feasibility — 7**
- Technical complexity vs. capability: 8 — Core strength; cheap multi-agent infra runs a single niche profitably; prototype already exists.

**Composite:** `0.40×7 + 0.35×7 + 0.25×7` = **7.0**

**De-risking step:** Hand-seed a single vertical the founder can already reach (e.g. one anime-lore or fantasy-football Discord) with 3–5 template agents; measure 4-week retained human followers and organic $5 spinoffs before touching a second niche.
